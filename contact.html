Customer Support & Orders FAQ

Everything you need to know regarding discreet shipping, returns, and ordering.

All VELORA orders arrive in an unmarked, tamper-evident courier box. The return address simply reads "V-Logistics Atelier" with zero mention of innerwear or lingerie. Inside, your items rest in an elegant matte black keepsake box tied with silk ribbon.
Orders placed prior to 2:00 PM CET ship the same business day via DHL Express or FedEx Air. Domestic deliveries arrive within 1–2 business days; international express takes 2–4 business days with end-to-end tracking.
Due to strict health and hygiene standards, items must be unworn, unwashed, with original tags and protective hygiene strips intact. However, under our First Pair Guarantee, you may trial your first pair of underwear completely risk-free: if the fit isn't right, keep it and we'll send a replacement size free of charge.
Because our fulfillment center operates rapidly, you have a 60-minute window following order confirmation to modify sizing or shipping addresses. Please telephone our concierge immediately or email concierge@velora-intimates.com with the subject line "URGENT ORDER UPDATE".
